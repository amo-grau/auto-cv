## Positioning: HALCON

**He does not want a job whose core is HALCON.** Stated 12.08.2026.

HALCON stays in the inventory — it is real experience and a week of training backs
it. But it is a capability, not a direction:

- Do not lead with it. Do not put it early in a Skills list.
- Do not target HALCON-centric or machine-vision-tooling roles.
- Include it where it is one competence among many, or where a posting names it as
  a nice-to-have.
- If a posting is built around HALCON, that is a signal the role is a poor fit —
  say so rather than tailoring hard for it.
